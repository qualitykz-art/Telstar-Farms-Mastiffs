About Telstar Farms Mastiffs

"We don't just breed dogs — we raise family members."

Welcome to Telstar Farms Mastiffs, a small, family-run English Mastiff breeding program nestled in the heart of Dixon, California. Our puppies are born and raised right here on our ranch, surrounded by wide-open spaces, children, and the everyday sounds and experiences of a real working home.

Every puppy in our program benefits from Early Neurological Stimulation (ENS) and Early Scent Introduction (ESI) protocols from day three of life. We believe in producing structurally sound, temperamentally excellent English Mastiffs that thrive as family companions, therapy dogs, and show prospects alike.

All of our breeding dogs are fully health-tested through OFA, ensuring that every puppy we produce has the best possible start. We are committed to this breed's future and to every family that joins the Telstar Farms family.

Health Tested Parents

Full OFA health panels including hips, elbows, cardiac, and thyroid testing on all breeding dogs. We leave nothing to chance.

Raised Ranch Life

Puppies grow up underfoot with children, other dogs, and daily ranch activities. They're socialized, confident, and ready for the world.

The Journey

Puppy Development Timeline

From birth to go-home day — here's what happens each week as your puppy grows with us on the ranch.

Week 0–1: Birth

Puppies are born! ENS (Early Neurological Stimulation) protocol begins on day 3. Daily weight checks ensure every puppy is thriving.

1
2

Week 1–2: Growing

Eyes and ears still closed. ENS protocol completes at day 16. Continued daily weight checks and gentle handling by our family.

Week 2–3: Eyes Open!

Eyes are opening! Puppies begin to walk, explore, and respond to their first sounds. The world gets a lot bigger.

3
4

Week 3–4: Weaning

Weaning begins! First solid food introduced. Socialization with household sounds, new textures, and gentle handling increases.

Week 4–5: Play Time

Play behavior is in full swing. Individual personalities emerge. First nail trims and grooming intro. So much fun to watch!

5
6

Week 5–6: Selection

Puppy selection week! Vet wellness check performed. First professional photos taken and shared with families.

Week 6–7: Prep

AKC registration processed. Microchipping completed. Deworming schedule continues. Puppies are getting ready!

7
8

Week 7–8: Final Prep

Final vet check, vaccine records completed, and preparing for the big day. Almost time to go home!

Week 8: GO HOME WEEK! 🎉

The day you've been waiting for! Your puppy comes home with a complete puppy packet, health records, AKC papers, starter food kit, blanket with littermate scent, and a lifetime of support from Telstar Farms.

Frequently Asked Questions

We welcome visits to the ranch for families on our waitlist once puppies are 5 weeks old and have had their first vet check. This protects the health of the puppies while they're still building their immune systems. We do offer video calls and regular photo/video updates before that time so you can watch your litter grow!

All of our breeding dogs undergo comprehensive OFA (Orthopedic Foundation for Animals) health testing, including: Hip Dysplasia evaluation, Elbow Dysplasia evaluation, Cardiac evaluation by a board-certified cardiologist, and Thyroid panel. We also perform DNA testing for breed-specific genetic conditions. All results are publicly available through the OFA database. We believe transparency in health testing is the foundation of responsible breeding.

Every Telstar Farms puppy goes home with: AKC registration papers (limited or full depending on your contract), a comprehensive puppy packet with feeding schedule and care instructions, vet health records including vaccinations and deworming history, a microchip (already registered to you), a starter bag of the food they've been eating, a blanket or toy with their littermates' scent for comfort, our 2-year genetic health guarantee, and a lifetime of support from us. We want the transition to be as smooth as possible for both you and your new puppy.

We strongly prefer in-person pickup from our ranch in Dixon, CA. We want to meet every family and ensure the transition is as comfortable as possible for the puppy. However, we understand that not everyone is local. For approved families outside of driving distance, we can arrange a professional pet nanny service at the buyer's expense. We do not cargo ship our puppies under any circumstances.

We feed a high-quality, large-breed-specific kibble and supplement with fresh, whole foods. Proper nutrition is critical for giant breed puppies — they need controlled, slow growth to protect their developing joints and bones. We'll send you home with a detailed feeding guide and a starter supply of the food your puppy has been eating so you can transition gradually if you choose a different brand.

We recommend waiting until your Mastiff is at least 18–24 months old to allow for proper growth and development. The timing will be outlined in your contract and we're happy to discuss the science behind this recommendation.

We offer a comprehensive 2-year genetic health guarantee on all of our puppies. This covers genetic conditions that significantly impact quality of life, including hip dysplasia, elbow dysplasia, and certain cardiac conditions. If your puppy is diagnosed with a covered condition within the guarantee period, we'll work with you on a resolution — which may include a replacement puppy from a future litter or a partial refund, depending on the situation. Full details are outlined in our purchase contract. Your puppy's health and your peace of mind are our top priority.
